## A. Velocity / pressure volume fields — what the numbers say

* **Aorta, steady, SSM-synthetic training (Liang 2020; Du 2022; Pajaziti 2023; Lin & Kenjereš 2025):** velocity 2–4 % and pressure 1.4–6 % normalised error on *synthetic* test shapes; on the only real-patient validation reported (Pajaziti, n = 10) pressure error rose to 10.19 % ± 10.41 (max case 23.6 % on synthetic), with ~60 % of the error attributable to SSM registration rather than the network. All four fix the inflow (1–1.3 m/s steady) and none conditions on BCs. Inference 1 ms – 1 s.
* **Coronary, steady, synthetic (Suk 2023; LaB-GATr 2024; AB-GATr 2026):** approximation error on volumetric velocity 7.4 % (SEGNN) → 3.3 % (LaB-GATr) → rel. L2 0.98 % (AB-GATr) on the same 2,000-case synthetic bifurcation family; NMAE ~0.7 %. Fixed BCs.
* **Coronary, pulsatile, real CCTA (Suk 2025, 74 patients):** approx. disparity 0.37 for velocity (MAE 0.189 m/s), 0.105 for pressure (MAE 1.0 kPa ≈ 7.5 mmHg), cos 0.76 — an order of magnitude worse than the synthetic steady results; BCs are inputs; needs a steady CFD prior.
* **Cerebral / aneurysm, transient (Maul 2023; Garnier 2025; Lannelongue 2026):** MAE 0.024 m/s (synthetic ICA trees); rollout RMSE 7.6 mm/s (~5–8 % relative) on semi-idealised aneurysms; 2.8 mm/s in-distribution → 5.4 mm/s OOD (MATCH) with ~10 % magnitude under-estimation. Inflow waveform/statistics are inputs in all three; Garnier and Lannelongue require a volume tetra mesh.
* **AAA volumetric velocity (AB-GATr 2026, AAA-100):** rel. L2 **39–42 %** for the best models and 109 % for a non-equivariant baseline — volumetric fields in real AAA anatomies remain unsolved at the field level, even though surface WSS on the same cohort reaches MAE 0.27–0.30 Pa (Rygiel 2025).
* **Real-cohort, imaging ground truth (Berhane 2025):** 1,765 patients, voxel bias < 0.01 m/s, LoA ±0.06–0.08 m/s, r² 0.93–0.96 for peak velocity/WSS, external r² 0.94–0.97 — but the target is 4D-flow MRI (systolic only), not CFD.
* **Named items not found:** "Kong et al. 2025" (only Kong et al. mesh-deformation segmentation cited by Image2Flow), "AneuNet", "MeshFlow", "Vascular flow neural field" (nearest: Suk 2025 conditional neural field; Lee & Schiavazzi 2025 INR), "Zhang et al. 2025 physics-informed coronary" (nearest: Moukneche 2026; Liu 2025 CorNet; Yang 2025), Maul/Siemens *coronary* FFR transient work (only the 2023 cerebral IPMI paper found), Transolver/GAOT/GNOT applied to patient-specific vascular flow (only as baselines: AB-UPT in AB-GATr; GNOT compared by Bafghi 2026; GeoTransolver in the automotive CP paper).

## C. Uncertainty estimation / calibration

Findings: **no vascular volumetric velocity/pressure surrogate found that reports calibrated predictive uncertainty (NLL heads, ensembles with coverage, or conformal intervals) on a held-out cohort.** Closest items:
* Rygiel et al. 2026 active learning (arXiv 2503.03453): MC-dropout query-by-committee used only to *select* CFD samples; no calibration reported.
* Gopakumar et al., "Uncertainty Quantification of Surrogate Models using Conformal Prediction", arXiv 2408.09881 / *Mach Learn Sci Technol* 2025-26: model-agnostic marginal-coverage guarantees for PDE/spatio-temporal surrogates; states MC dropout and deep ensembles often need further calibration — generic, no vascular case.
* Jia et al. 2026, arXiv 2607.17297: multi-granularity conformal prediction on GeoTransolver/Transolver **automotive** surface pressure & WSS; 90 % nominal coverage; interval width −22.68 % (pressure), −25.35 … −27.09 % (WSS); coverage SD 10.41 → 3.10 pp with out-of-fold calibration — a transferable recipe, not a vascular result.
* Sperrer 2025 (TU Wien thesis): heteroscedastic outputs + ensembles for DL-ROM CFD **(generic; details unverified)**.
* Menon et al. 2024, arXiv 2409.02247: Bayesian BC estimation + multi-fidelity UQ for coronary CFD — physics-based UQ, no DL surrogate.
* DeepFFR 2025 mentions UQ methods **(details unverified)**.

## D. Data size / learning-curve statements

* Suk 2023: on 2,000 synthetic coronaries, 160 → 16 training meshes doubles PointNet++ ε (10.9 → 23.9 %) and raises SEGNN ε 1.5× (9.3 → 14.1 %); "SEGNN favourable for clinical trials with < 160 patients"; "good accuracy when trained on 160 instead of 1,600".
* Du 2022: training ratio 60 → 90 % of 1,000 synthetic aortas changes velocity MSE only 0.735 → 0.64 % (surface pressure 1.83 → 1.48 %).
* Rygiel 2026 (active learning): disparity 0.146 (128 random) vs 0.117 (1,000) — the last 8× data buys 0.03 disparity; physics-adherence querying halves samples.
* Nannini 2025: 427 patient-specific simulations from 105 geometries judged "insufficient" — only LaB-GATr learned the patient data (11.4 ± 6.5 Pa vs 13–15 Pa for others).
* Garnier 2025: empirical scaling law P ∝ C^0.75 from 60 model variants on 147 simulations (2.85 B node-timestep pairs).
* Pegolotti 2024: errors < 2 % / 3 % "when adequate training data"; plans "more patient-specific geometries with fewer trajectories each".
* Pajaziti 2023: no learning-curve analysis; synthetic→real error roughly doubles for pressure.
* Typical dataset sizes: real geometries **25–150** (aorta/coronary/AAA/aneurysm) expanded to **700–10,660 synthetic**; the largest real cohorts are Berhane 2025 (1,765, MRI GT), Yang 2025 (370, centerline pressure), Suk 2026 carotid (282 in-vivo), Versnjak 2024 (143 + 85), Nannini 2025 (105 patients / 427 sims), Rygiel 2025 (100 + 29 external), Suk 2025 (74).

## F. AAA / aorto-iliac

* Closest anatomy match: **Rygiel et al. 2025** (100 AAA patients incl. 5 cm of both iliacs; surface WSS/TAWSS/OSI; external 118 scans; MAE 0.27–0.30 Pa; inflow as input; mesh-resolution-agnostic; surface mesh only). **AB-GATr 2026** reports the only volumetric velocity result on the same AAA-100: rel. L2 39–42 % (best) — i.e., AAA *volume* fields are far from solved.
* Faisal 2025 (23 real + 230 synthetic AAA, TAWSS NMAE 0.362 %); Cruz-González 2025 (single idealised AAA, PINN/DeepONet, rel. L2 ~1–3 % across inlet velocities); Robles Arques 2026 (patient AAA PINN, no CFD ground truth).
* Aorto-iliac / aortofemoral appear only as reduced-order (Rubio 2026: 17 aortofemoral VMR models, MPE 8.1 → 3.7 %; Pegolotti 2024: 1 aortofemoral aneurysm model). **No 3-D volumetric velocity/pressure surrogate trained on a real aorto-iliac cohort was found.**

## Gaps (methodological)
1. Volumetric velocity/pressure surrogates on *real* aortic/AAA anatomies: only Pajaziti (10 real, p 10 %), Suk 2025 (74 coronaries, disparity 0.37), AB-GATr AAA-100 (rel. L2 ~40 %). No aorto-iliac cohort.
2. BC conditioning: most aortic works fix inflow; BC-as-input is standard only in the Twente/Mines-Paris lines (Suk 2025, Maul 2023, Garnier, Lannelongue, Rygiel AAA, Nannini) and in centerline/0-D models.
3. Uncertainty: no calibrated predictive uncertainty on vascular volume fields; conformal recipes exist only for generic PDE / automotive surrogates.
4. Point-cloud-only inference exists (Li 2021, Maul 2023, Suk 2025/2026, PointNet++ lines, LaB-GATr) but most transient GNN work still needs a CFD-quality volume mesh; Suk 2025 additionally needs a steady CFD prior.
5. Real-cohort scale: 25–150 real geometries is typical; learning curves (Suk 2023, Du 2022, Rygiel 2026) show diminishing but non-zero returns; Nannini calls 427 patient simulations insufficient; prospective validation absent.
